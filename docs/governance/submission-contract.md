# Submission contract

A public change MUST be submitted as a focused commit or pull request with a clear purpose, tests or validation appropriate to its scope, and no private material.

Contributors MUST preserve the normative meaning of the contracts. If a change alters a rule, it MUST update the relevant document and explain the compatibility impact in the change description.

A proposal MAY introduce a new rule, content, or feature package, but implementation MUST NOT be implied by proposal text. Proposals MUST identify affected contracts, player-facing risk, migration needs, and whether the change is reversible.

Automated checks are required but are not sufficient for governance review. Maintainers MAY reject a technically valid change that violates the pillars, anti-goals, protected boundaries, or public/private boundary.
